## Finding the Right Light

The best time to shoot in Mumbai is during the golden hour—either right after sunrise or just before sunset. The way the warm light bounces off colonial buildings in Kala Ghoda or the Gateway of India is truly magical.

### Equipment

While I love my modern gear, there's a certain charm to using an old digicam. My trusted **DC403 Digicam** gives these photos a nostalgic, CCD-sensor feel that modern phones often over-process.

## Conclusion

Just keep walking. The best frames aren't planned; they happen when you are completely present in the moment.
